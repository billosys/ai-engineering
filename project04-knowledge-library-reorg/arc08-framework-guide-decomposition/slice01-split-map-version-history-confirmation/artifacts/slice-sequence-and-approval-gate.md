# Slice Sequence and Approval Gate

## Approval Gate

Slice02 must not open until operator approval of
`artifacts/operator-confirmation-packet.md` is recorded.

Expedited Mode does not override this gate. It does not authorize source
decomposition, inferred source scope, skipped validation, weaker evidence, or a
timeline-driven interpretation of the plan.

## Slice Sequence

1. Slice01: confirmation packet and source-impact plan. No source edits.
2. Slice02: after operator approval, correct Expedited Mode wording and move
   project-management `version-history.md` to the component sibling location.
3. Slice03: split collaboration-framework posture material into the four
   approved guides.
4. Slice04: split engineering-methods methodology material into the six
   accepted guides.
5. Slice05: normalize remaining framework component version-history files.
6. Slice06: final package, install, link, CCDP, and release reconciliation.

## Source Decomposition Boundary

No source decomposition may begin until the operator confirms the split map,
sibling version-history rule, and Expedited Mode wording target. If the
operator adjusts the packet, Arc08 planning must be updated before Slice02
opens.

## Required Operator Record

The next step is an operator decision recorded in planning evidence. Acceptable
forms:

- confirm the packet as written;
- confirm with named adjustments;
- reject and request a revised packet.

Until then, Slice02 remains blocked by the approval gate.
