## Review Procedure

1. Open the source locator in each support record against the pinned checkout
   identified in [source acquisition](./source-acquisition.md).
2. Compare each candidate claim and summary to its selected source span,
   including retained qualifications and excluded conclusions.
3. Confirm that direct figure inspection and unresolved citations or
   cross-references are correctly described; do not infer support from a
   locator, bibliography key, or figure reference alone.
4. Record an explicit decision and rationale per candidate. A decision to
   retain a candidate is not independent verification or memory admission
   unless a separate scoped record establishes either one.

## Review Evidence To Return

later Slice03 must dispose of the friction findings regardless of whether the
