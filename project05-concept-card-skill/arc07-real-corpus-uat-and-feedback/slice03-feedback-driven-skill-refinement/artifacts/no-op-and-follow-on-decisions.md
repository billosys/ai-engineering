# No-Op And Follow-On Decisions

## Checked No-Ops

F-1 and F-3 through F-7 are checked no-ops. Their live-source evidence and
applied pilot behavior are recorded in [finding disposition](./finding-disposition.md).
They do not mean the pilot data is unimportant; they mean the current source
guidance already supplied the needed rule and a duplicate rule, template, or
example would add no demonstrated capability.

## Follow-On Boundaries

No Slice02 finding becomes a new follow-on from this Slice03 disposition.
