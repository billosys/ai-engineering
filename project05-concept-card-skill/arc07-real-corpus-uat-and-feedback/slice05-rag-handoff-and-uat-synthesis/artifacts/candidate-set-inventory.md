## Shared Lifecycle Boundary

Every listed card has `card_status: candidate-requires-operator-review`.
All ten are tied to pinned commit
`e0c697b4d4d134f29cb5c2bbde41b1b6b409b63d`; Slice02 retains richer linked
claim/support records while Slice04 records claim-specific support comparisons
inline. That shape difference is provenance to retain, not a license to infer
missing lifecycle fields or flatten support into unqualified text.

For the full set, operator acceptance, independent semantic verification,
reconciliation, preservation, and memory admission are unassessed. Slice04
records state this directly. Slice02 records carry the same boundary through
their lifecycle fields and review packet. `self-checked` validation on the
Slice04 records is extraction self-checking, not independent verification.
