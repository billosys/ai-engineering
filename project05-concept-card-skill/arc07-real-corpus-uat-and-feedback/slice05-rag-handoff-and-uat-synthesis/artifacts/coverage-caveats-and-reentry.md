## Full-Book Re-Entry Condition

A later, explicitly scoped effort may re-enter full-book extraction only after
it defines, before generation: chapter-by-chapter coverage targets; figure and
citation dependency sampling; reviewer capacity and decision workflow;
validation sampling; source snapshot handling; and the treatment of unresolved
bibliography and cross-reference dependencies. It must retain the same
candidate/lifecycle boundary unless a new plan supplies separate review and
verification evidence.
