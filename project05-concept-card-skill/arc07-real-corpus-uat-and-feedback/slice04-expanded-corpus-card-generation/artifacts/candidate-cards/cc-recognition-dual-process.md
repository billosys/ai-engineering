# Recognition: Familiarity And Recollection

## Boundary

This card captures the source's contrast between perirhinal familiarity and
hippocampal recollection. It excludes a claim that the model is settled beyond
what the textbook reports.

## Claim And Source Support

The source describes a coarse graded familiarity signal associated with
perirhinal cortex, contrasted with hippocampal recollection of episode details,
and calls the combination a dual-process recognition-memory model.
