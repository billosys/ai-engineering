# Priming: Weight-Based And Activation-Based

## Boundary

This card narrows to priming's two proposed mechanisms and relative duration.
It excludes experimental paradigms and claims about broad synaptic theory.

## Claim And Source Support

The source distinguishes priming due to residual activation from priming due
to longer-lived weight changes; it reports that activation-based priming fades
with neural activity while weight-based priming can persist much longer.
