## Gap And Carry Forward

Arc07 was correctly narrow and candidate-only, but it revealed that the live template does not default to a body that helps a reader define, recognize, apply, compare, and avoid misuse of a concept. Carry forward: quick and core definitions; prerequisites; enumerated properties; construction/recognition; context/application; source-specific examples; explained relationships; common errors; common confusions; source route; and extraction/review notes.

The v4 adaptation is decisive: claims and source support remain authoritative; relationship/CQ prose cannot invent an edge or answerability claim; examples must have locators; and extraction/review notes cannot substitute for result records or lifecycle states.

## Retired Or Unsafe Behavior

- The old `Verification Notes` mixed confidence, synthesis, cross-reference, preservation, and review. Split those concerns across v4 extraction notes, source support, validation, verification, reconciliation, preservation, and admission records.
- Do not restore old page-only provenance, one confidence-as-approval field, bare relationship lists, source-preparation ownership, or repository paths.
- The v3.2 prompts require a search for `Wait`, `Let me recalculate`, `I apologize`, and `As an AI` generation artifacts (0009 line 628; 0010 lines 707-720). Slice03 must reject conversational wrappers and whole-card fenced response envelopes, not preserve them as card content.
- Richness does not imply operator acceptance, independent semantic verification, reconciliation, preservation, memory admission, full-book coverage, retrieval quality, or graph/RAG/MCP runtime behavior.
