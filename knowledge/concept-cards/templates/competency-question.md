# Competency Question: <short label>

Use the [template conventions](../SKILL.md#record-templates) and
[CQ procedure](../guides/06-graph-cq.md#establish-a-competency-question).
Record requirement, coverage, answerability, verification or retrieval roles
explicitly. Draft question status is not a judgment of coverage or answerability.

## Requirement And Answer Criteria

<State the originating question/source scope and intended use. Identify the
components, distinctions, qualifications and evidence needed for an adequate
answer. Retaining a requirement does not endorse an answer.>

## Coverage Assertions And Evidence

<Complete one identifiable coverage assertion per component, with exact covered
card/claim/support/edge revisions and rationale. The embedded assertion's ID
and revision allow support and result records to target it separately from
the CQ. Record partial, conflicting, absent or inaccessible evidence.>

## Answerability And Retrieval Observations

<Attempt the bounded answer from mapped material and explain unsupported steps
or assumptions. Record suitability for intended use separately from coverage.
If a retrieval probe actually occurred, name query, searched snapshot/method,
returned constructs and observations; otherwise leave it unassessed. Retrieval
success is not verification or memory admission, and failure is not proof that
the source lacks an answer. Source cleanup belongs to document-extraction.>

## Changed, Obsolete Or Deferred Question

<Preserve prior text, origin, coverage and results; explain revision/replacement,
obsolete rationale or deferred work with actor/decision and re-entry condition.
Obsolete does not mean answered. Do not inflate coverage by deleting hard CQs.>

## Lifecycle And Handoff

<Identify validation, verification, reconciliation and preservation at their
actual scope, including results targeting particular coverage assertions.
For admission, specify whether the target is the requirement, coverage or
answerability assertion. Report unassessed work, source/run identity, observer
provenance, access/storage limits and next checks without claiming runtime use.>
