# Preservation Decision: <prior value>

Use the [template conventions](../SKILL.md#record-templates) and
[preservation procedure](../guides/04-re-extraction-preservation.md#record-preservation-decisions).
Target cards, claims, annotations, support, edges, CQs or prior results as needed.
Group targets only when each is named and the same rationale applies to all.

## Prior Value And Source Basis

<Identify exact prior revisions and unique definitions, explanations, examples,
qualifications or annotations. Compare against the source-derived candidate;
prior cards are secondary inputs, not substitutes for the source. Distinguish
missing, inaccessible and inspected-but-unsupported evidence. Retain
document-extraction provenance/caveats if used; source cleanup belongs there.>

## Disposition And Destination

<Choose preserved, superseded, rejected or unresolved with evidence and rationale.
Preserved names the active or historical destination; superseded names the
replacement and retained old history; rejected records exclusion and keeps its
trail; unresolved names what is undecided and a re-entry condition. Split/merge
operations need old-to-new mappings and per-value dispositions, not silent loss.
An admission rejection is not an instruction to delete preserved artifacts.>

## Lifecycle And Acceptance Implications

<Record affected claims, support, edges, CQs and result applicability. Preservation
does not establish support or settle conceptual conflict; identify separate
reconciliation work. Retaining an old admission record is not new admission.
Set operator_review_required from actual requirements, with rationale, and cite
existing applicable acceptance or missing review; do not invent a blanket gate.>

## Handoff And Remaining Work

<State whether the disposition is proposed or applied, output locations/revisions,
actor/run and direct versus reported observations. Preserve earlier decisions,
unavailable inputs and uninspected dependencies. Name next checks and delivery
limits; a shorter new artifact is not proof that prior value survived.>
