# Methodology Stewardship Version History

## Version 1.2.0 - 2026-10-19

Add reconstruction-oriented research-record evaluation and bounded follow-through: original attempts and decisions, model/configuration provenance, portability/access limits and actual operational observations. Preserve full ledger quality and avoid inferring efficiency, multi-year durability or causal model benefits from record size or unrun trials.

## Version 1.1.0 - 2026-10-18

Extended mandatory CDC slice, arc and project retrospectives to Autonomous
Mode regardless of contributor arrangement; preserved evidence, independence
and recommendation authority.

## Version 1.0.0 - 2026-10-12

Introduced the skill and supporting retrospective guide. Defines observation,
scale-specific synthesis, contributor-inclusive diagnosis, artifact utility,
and recommendation follow-through. CDC owns closing retrospectives at every
scale in the Three-Contributor Workflow; acceptance and change authority remain
with their existing workflow owners. Effectiveness claims require evidence,
not retrospective confidence alone.
