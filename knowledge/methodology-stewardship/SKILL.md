# Methodology Stewardship

Improve how the collaboration works by connecting observed difficulties and
successes to subsequent decisions. Include planning, implementation, review,
handoffs and evidence maintenance; no contributor is exempt from analysis.

Read [Retrospectives and Follow-through](./guides/01-retrospectives-and-follow-through.md)
when observing execution, closing a slice/arc/project, synthesizing repeated
patterns, or checking an earlier recommendation's outcome. It covers evidence,
record shape, scale-specific questions, symptoms and recommendation handling.

In the Three-Contributor Workflow and Autonomous Mode, CDC owns a `cdc-retrospective.md` in each
closing slice, arc and project root. Follow project-management's closing and
handoff contract; this is a learning record, not another acceptance verdict.
The skill can support an explicitly requested retrospective in other workflows
without inventing contributors or making this record mandatory there.

Use scientific-methods when a recommendation becomes a trial or a comparative
claim. Use the existing planning and verification skills for scope decisions,
authority and correctness findings. Observing a pattern does not authorize a
framework change or establish that an intervention works.
