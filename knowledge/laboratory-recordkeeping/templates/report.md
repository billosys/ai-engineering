## Attempt accounting

Record planned slots, all execution attempts, retries, usable observations,
exclusions with reasons, and missing/not-run slots. Link the complete run index.
Explain the denominator for every rate or aggregate.

## Reconstruction record

State what is documented, what was inspected, what was actually re-executed,
by whom, whether independently, and how it compared. Link the corresponding
run records. Name inaccessible inputs and deviations; do not imply a rerun
occurred when only a recipe was checked.

## What remains unproven or unexamined

State alternative explanations, scope limits, unanswered questions, and the
next discriminating test. Record any decision to stop without resolving them.

## Amendments

Add dated corrections or successor-report links and their reasons. Preserve
earlier findings and connect affected summaries to the correction.
