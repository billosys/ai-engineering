## Access and retention

For each external or restricted item, record:

- original source and original filename;
- retrieval instructions and access conditions;
- license or other reuse terms;
- storage and retention owner;
- independent backup destination and last retrieval check;
- availability gaps, planned retention end, or unknown arrangements.

Do not put credentials in locators or instructions. For derived artifacts,
link source inventory IDs, generating code and configuration, and run/analysis
identity. Record removals, migrations, and replacement locators with dates and
reasons. Preserve the identity of what an earlier run actually used.
