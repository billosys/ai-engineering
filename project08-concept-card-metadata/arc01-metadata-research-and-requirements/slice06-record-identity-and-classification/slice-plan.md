# Record Identity And Classification

Deliver contextual evidence for 37 observed pairs: the identity/revision and
type of current non-card records, plus legacy discovery classification and
pedagogical tier. This bounded sibling remediation supplies part of Slice04's
original obligation; it does not replace or close that slice's ledger.

## Dependencies And Scope

Read the project and arc plans/ledgers, Project08 AGENTS.md, and the accepted
Slice04 Batch01 registry, report and latest CDC review. Slice01's mechanical
inventory remains the count/shape input, not semantic authority. Source baseline
is `e763c661`; Batch01 semantic delivery is `995c86d6`, with its replay
documentation subsequently corrected by CDC. Record actual current bases.

The exact selected pairs are:

- Root `id` and root `revision` for each of these eleven observed kinds:
  claim, competency-question, extraction-run, memory-admission,
  preservation-decision, reconciliation-result, relationship-edge,
  source-locator, source-support, validation-result, verification-result.
  This is 22 pairs; concept-card id/revision already belong to accepted Batch01.
- Root `record_type` for those eleven kinds plus concept-card: 12 pairs.
- `category`, `subcategory` and `tier` in `untyped`: three pairs.

No nested IDs, actor identities, lifecycle decision meanings, source locators,
relationship predicates or new graph schema enter this slice merely because
they occur in the same records. Inspect them for necessary context only.
Do not confuse a record's identity with its subject/target or an assessment
with the construct it assesses. Type labels are representation discriminators,
not an accepted ontology taxonomy.

For classification, compare actual historical domain vocabulary and the
documented meaning of prerequisite depth. Distinguish classification from
pedagogical dependency and source coordinates. Preserve evidence of observed
inconsistency rather than silently repairing a legacy card. Do not impose the
old three fields on future domains or choose a replacement standard here.
