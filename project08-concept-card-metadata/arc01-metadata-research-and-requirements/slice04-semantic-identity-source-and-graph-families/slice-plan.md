# Semantic Identity, Source And Graph Families

Replace the generic Slice01 annotations for the assigned families with authored,
evidence-backed meanings and concrete old/current capability comparisons. This
is the first of two planned sibling remediation slices, not standards research
or a metadata schema decision.

Read the project plan and ledger, arc plan and ledger, Project08 AGENTS.md,
and Slice01's
[remediation decision](../slice01-metadata-inventory-and-research-questions/artifacts/remediation-decomposition.md).

## In Scope

- Concept identity and labels, classification and pedagogical progression.
- Bibliographic identity; source snapshots, representations, locators and spans.
- Claim/support structure, relationship predicates/endpoints, and competency
  questions with coverage assertions.
- Exact authored membership by normalized path and observed record context;
  distinguish historical/current representations and corpus-specific meanings.
- Concrete body/metadata, query and migration consequences, including uncertainty,
  omissions, examples and failures. Generality beyond book-shaped sources is a
  comparison question, not an assumed property of present records.
- Exact inspected-input registration, proposed research questions, and an
  enumerated remainder for Slice05 under the accepted family split.

Slice05 owns run/actor/worker provenance, evidence/confidence, validation/
verification, reconciliation, preservation, admission/operator acceptance,
the final union/join, full replay and original Slice01 handoff reconciliation.
Cross-family fields require explicit primary ownership and cross-references;
do not treat every nested `id` or `state` as the same kind of information.

## Out Of Scope

No source-skill edits, schema adoption, package/install action, new corpus
extraction, production query service, card source-support verification or
memory admission. No edits to Slice01's evidence, helpers, fixtures, reviewer
records or baseline copies. Slice05 will integrate the accepted packets.
Do not implement the final join or spend this semantic slice on parser repairs.

## Work And Artifacts

Artifacts belong in this slice's `artifacts/`:

1. `input-register.md`: exact paths, inspected sections, hashes, revision bases,
   availability and observed limitations for definitions and representative data.
   Read both v3.2 prompts in full; inspect relevant predecessor differences.
2. `semantic-families.md`: authored definitions, actual examples, old/current
   dispositions, source anchors, concrete lookup/migration consequences, body
   interactions and open research questions. Split families by meaning as needed.
3. `semantic-membership.json`: explicit assigned field-path/context memberships
   linked to authored family IDs and supporting evidence. Document this planning
   artifact's structure; do not present it as the future concept-card schema.
4. `remainder-membership.json`: enumerated observed pairs assigned to Slice05,
   with intended family and reason. Includes no claim of completed semantics.
5. `handoff.md`: research implications, cross-family interfaces, concrete
   Slice05 integration/review needs, unresolved findings and sizing observations.
6. `validation-evidence.md`: exact commands and results for JSON parsing,
   membership coverage, input identity checks, semantic spot checks and hygiene.

First size the assigned families with review headroom. If another split is
needed, return the specific family/criterion remainder for a plan amendment;
do not shrink the deliverables or fill them with generated generic prose.

## Verification And Exit

The recorded census has 308 paths and 555 observed path/kind pairs. Independently
derive those pairs from the index and compare the union of assigned/remainder
memberships: no omission, nonexistent pair or unexplained overlap. Include
containers and null/empty paths. Further corpus/role distinctions must map back
to these pairs, not disappear behind `untyped` or record-kind aggregation.

Resolve every assigned membership to an authored family and evidence. Review
definitions, dispositions and real body/value comparisons across every assigned
family and each claimed preservation category. A missing definition is a
documented searched-scope ambiguity, not license to invent its meaning.
Do not infer machine-query equivalence merely from a prose mention or reference.

Keep the census/baseline bytes unchanged. Check new JSON with an established
parser, all cited local targets and hashes, planning whitespace and source
status before/after. No package gates are required for this planning-only work.
The eight-row sibling ledger governs this slice only. CC proposes completion;
CDC independently verifies it and sizes/opens Slice05. This slice alone does
not close Slice01 or establish full semantic coverage.
