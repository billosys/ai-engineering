## Bounded proposed decomposition

Do not advance to Slice02. Keep the current Slice01 ledger rows open and add
the following bounded remediation slices to Arc01 after operator/CDC planning
review:

1. `slice01a-semantic-identity-source-and-graph-families`: concept identity and
   labels; classification/progression; bibliographic identity; source snapshots,
   representations, locators/spans; claims/support; relationships; competency
   questions and coverage assertions. Deliver authored family definitions,
   memberships and source anchors for those families.
2. `slice01b-semantic-lifecycle-provenance-and-replay`: run/actor/worker
   provenance; evidence/confidence; validation/verification; reconciliation;
   preservation; admission/operator acceptance; the Fennel mechanical-to-authored
   join/check; and a literal macOS Bash 3.2 replay against all nine roots.

The split is by authority and evidence surface, not by field-name suffix. It
retains all 308 paths and their current record contexts. The shared acceptance
boundary is a complete authored membership map plus a reproduced join/coverage
check; neither child may claim Arc02 schema selection or semantic source
verification outside its named evidence.
