# Slice02 Research Agenda

Priorities are derived from observed gaps and the operator brief. Standards are
research candidates, not selections or compliance claims.

| Priority | Research question and candidates | Required handoff / probe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Smallest portable profile that retains historical discovery/classification without forcing category/subcategory/tier? Research SKOS and application-profile practice. | Field-authority alternatives with music, Erlang, paper, white-paper, blog and encyclopedia fixtures; historical values recoverable. |
| P0 | How separate resource, edition/version, mutable capture, prepared representation and cited span? Research Dublin Core terms/application profiles, BIBFRAME and PROV-O. | Identity/locator matrix for book page, paper section, captured URL and no-page/no-chapter source; unknowns remain explicit. |
| P0 | How retain directly traversable prerequisite/extends/related/contrast semantics with support, uncertainty and revisions? Research SKOS and domain predicates separately. | Local harness for fugue prerequisites, extension, contrast, related; tests missing endpoint, cycle, reverse direction and uncertain edge. |
| P0 | What distinguishes CQ text, identity, coverage and answerability? Consult NeON or other ontology requirements-engineering primary material. | CQ fixtures with identity, covered components/support and unassessed versus partial answerability. |
| P0 | Which lifecycle summaries are safe without card-wide promotion? | Mixed-state fixture: supported/unverified, verified, conflict, stale and no admission; every summary names subject/revision/actor/authority. |
| P1 | How synchronize a readable card projection with authoritative edge/CQ/support records? | Authority and stale-projection rule; changed-edge and missing-record fixture. |
| P1 | Which rich-body requirements are source-independent? | Anchored rubric for definition, recognition/construction, examples, errors, relationship usefulness and source fidelity; not heading-count theatre. |
| P1 | What parser/reference validation is needed? | Colon-scalar regression fixtures from preserved rich failures; null/empty/missing and ID/revision/path checks. |
| P2 | How migrate flat strings and absent `extends`? | Complete legacy value-shape preservation table with re-entry conditions. |

For every candidate standard, Slice02 must record primary source/release identity,
exact applicable clause, benefit, cost, YAML portability, alternative and
non-adopted parts. Naming a standard is not selecting it.

## Arc02 decision agenda

Identity/source/locator layers; classification versus pedagogical progression;
edge/CQ authority and projection; lifecycle scope; migration form; validator and
fixtures; rich-body rubric and repeated-run protocol. This slice decides none.
