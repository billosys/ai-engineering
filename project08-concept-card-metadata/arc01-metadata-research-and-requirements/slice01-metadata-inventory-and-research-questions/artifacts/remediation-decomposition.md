paths and 13 record-kind labels. Summing each path's observed `record_kinds`
list yields 555 path/kind pairs. These are not 308 interchangeable contexts.

historical cards, synthetic examples or other untyped records.

Slice04 records an explicit partition: memberships analyzed now, and the
enumerated remainder owned by Slice05 with reasons. This is an ownership
register, not a semantic-completion claim for the remainder. Split observed
contexts further where source generation, record role or actual meaning
differs. Assert coverage of all mechanical pairs, containers and null/empty
paths. Do not assume all 308 paths occur in all 13 kinds.

suffixes. Slice05 checks the final union for gaps, extras, contradictory or
unexplained duplicate meanings, and unresolved family references. Mechanical
coverage does not establish that an authored disposition is correct.
