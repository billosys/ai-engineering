The following **tested jq predicate fragment** is a starting point for the
native census, not a full route or a substitute for member interpretation.
CRC ran its equivalent at the authoring baseline and observed `true`/status
0 for 37/12/4/21. Keep mode and role separate, and extend it to per-kind,
family, parse-error and legacy assertions. A different local function shape
is allowed if it preserves the same contract and controls.

~~~jq
def state($v;$f):
  if ($v|has("actor")|not) then "parent-absent"
  elif $v.actor==null then "parent-null"
  elif ($v.actor|type)!="object" then "parent-unexpected"
  elif ($v.actor|has($f)|not) then "child-absent"
  elif $v.actor[$f]==null then "child-null"
  else "value:"+($v.actor[$f]|tojson) end;
[$inventory[0].records[]
 | select(.record_kind|IN("claim","competency-question","concept-card","extraction-run"))
 | select(.values|type=="object")
 | {kind:.record_kind, path, mode:state(.values;"mode"), role:state(.values;"role")}] as $rows
| ($rows|length)==37
  and ([$rows[]|select(.mode=="parent-absent" and .role=="parent-absent")]|length)==12
  and ([$rows[]|select(.mode=="child-null" and .role=="child-null")]|length)==4
  and ([$rows[]|select(.mode=="value:\"agent-direct\"" and .role=="value:\"extractor\"")]|length)==21
~~~
