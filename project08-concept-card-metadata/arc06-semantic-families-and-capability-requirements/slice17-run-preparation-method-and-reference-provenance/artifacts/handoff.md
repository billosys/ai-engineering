## Interpretation conflicts and limits

- The current template documents plural prepared-source collections, while the
  synthetic examples document singular prepared-source mappings. Both surfaces
  are retained; no equivalence, migration or requiredness decision is made.
- Current guidance is source-primary and distinguishes preparation provenance
  from assertion support. Synthetic bodies say the prepared snapshot is
  consumed, but the declared paths are not present in the source checkout; the
  example cannot be promoted into a resolved target or claim support.
- Historical v3.2 material describes provenance, re-extraction and fixed
  parallel assignments. Current guidance does not require five workers, and
  historical prompts do not instantiate current extraction-run fields.
- Template nulls and empty collections are observed placeholders. Synthetic
  absence is not evidence of global omission, and parsed legacy absence is
  bounded to the Complete Musician/Erlang comparison population.
