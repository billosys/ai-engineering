# Unified Design Practice — 2.0 Target

## Purpose and authority

Develop the design skill into a designer's working resource across intent,
content, perception, expression, interaction and implementation. Preserve
intentional relationships, content-led composition, contextual expression and
craft. Initial operational scope remains web, product and editorial design.
Full native-platform specialization, print, spatial/industrial design and full
service-design practice remain future extensions requiring scope decisions.

Plan revision **1.1**: the Operator requested assessment of Project10's delivery
amendment `fdb073f5` and a streamlined Project11 plan that lands useful changes
while retaining quality. This revision replaces the initial seven discipline
arcs with four delivery arcs, preserving all seven capabilities and ten project
criteria. No arc or slice has been opened, and no implementation prompt is issued.
Planning remains on `planning`; authorized source/package edits belong in the
source checkout. This amendment changes Project11, not Project10's active work.

The shared [Project09 audit](../project09-design-audit/artifacts/audit-results-design.md)
and [release synthesis](../project09-design-audit/artifacts/audit-modernization-synthesis.md)
remain provenance, not current observations of the corrected skill. Consume
[Project10's actual accepted results](../project10-design-foundations/project-plan.md)
and pin the relevant revisions. Its full-corpus correction, metadata work and
source-fidelity obligations remain there; Project11 does not repeat them or
silently claim them complete. The [ledger](ledger.md) governs final acceptance.

  These are concrete source changes; they do not yet prove lower total effort

  deleted protected teaching/CSS and left example/route inconsistencies. The
  independent review caught them. Faster edits still need positive requirements
  for what remains useful, not only scans for what must disappear.

Project11's original roadmap had good capability criteria, late detailed planning
and an explicit warning that more reading alone is insufficient. Its risk was
structural: seven mostly discipline-based arcs, broad upstream dependencies,
source mutation left implicit, and knowledge/package composition concentrated
at the end. The changes below remove that opportunity for evidence-only phases
without reducing the original capabilities, expert review or release gates.

## Delivery contract for every new slice

A normal slice lands one useful change in `knowledge/design/`: an actionable
method, corrected/extended guide, runnable example, or task route. Update the
entrypoint and build payload when needed so the changed capability is available
from the isolated package in that same slice. A planning packet alone is not a
normal delivery outcome. “Landed” means a source commit integrated into the
agreed implementation baseline and independently accepted, not merely proposed
done or an unmerged draft. It does not mean publication of a release.

The open set names the task served, exact source paths/revision, intended before/
after behavior, protected teaching/examples/identity, and the smallest adequate
checks. Research is driven by a question needed for that change. Inspect relevant
primary sources and attribution; do not require a comprehensive framework survey
or a new corpus before a bounded supported improvement. Borrow methods without
importing corporate identities or universalizing a framework's preferences.

Use a difficult representative example when uncertainty warrants it, and exercise
its complete path through the final output. Then deliver the bounded batch;
avoid example-only preparation that never reaches implementation. Keep existing
working snippets, routes and accepted corrections unless their deliberate change
is in scope and its replacement is reviewed. Check meaningful neighboring claims,
links and dependencies, not just edited strings or schema agreement.

An investigation-only slice is exceptional: name the specific decision/input
blocking a responsible edit, the bounded answer needed, the intended source
consumer and a stop/re-entry condition. An unchanged missing input does not
justify repeated readiness work. Prefer the next ready delivery when the answer
is unavailable. Do not manufacture edits to make a research assignment look useful.

Keep intake, failures, coverage and evidence in the canonical records and normally
one concise batch note; additional artifacts need a distinct consumer or check.
Use source diffs, runnable examples and existing tools as evidence. Do not add
receipt-only acceptance rows, duplicate datasets or bespoke verifier frameworks
unless an actual consequential property needs them. Persistent failure triggers
bounded diagnosis and a concrete repair demonstration, not a longer restatement
of the prompt. Preserve the existing iteration limit and independent review.

## Readiness and coordination with Project10

**Was:** exploratory planning could proceed, but implementation waited for whole
Project10 acceptance. **Now:** a Project11 slice may consume an independently
accepted Project10 increment when its particular foundation inputs and contracts
are sufficient. P11-01 and final release readiness still require the accepted
complete Project09/10 handoffs and disposition of all inherited limitations.
A current main HEAD, inventory, partial verdict or deferred source gap is not
blanket foundation acceptance.

At intake pin the current source and the exact acceptance evidence used. Check
which claims/files are accepted and whether subsequent changes invalidate that
evidence. Unresolved source fidelity blocks claims that rely on it; do not reuse
an uncertain historical assertion as verified teaching. Unrelated supported work
can proceed. Existing Project10 responsibilities stay owned there, without a
second full 233-card certification here. New/changed cards follow the accepted
concept-card contract and get claim-level review for their actual changes.

While both projects are active, CDC coordinates file ownership and mutation
windows before issuing overlapping source assignments. Use an isolated checkout
if needed; name the target integration baseline and require rebase/drift review
before acceptance there. Do not run concurrent edits to the same guide or move
another assignment's pin silently. No Project11 source work is opened by this
plan amendment alone.

## Evaluation alongside delivery

Use the same growing set of task specimens across slices. Before each relevant
implementation, declare its brief/content, supplied identity, expected success
and credible failures, and applicable browser/input/locale conditions. Store
runnable shipped examples in the package's supported payload; use slice artifacts
for the compact observation record. Do not make users load research inventories.

| Task family retained | First delivery owner / necessary observation |
|---|---|
| Editorial long-form | Arc01; hierarchy, meaningful alternatives/revision, links, reading measure, enlarged text and font failure. Arc03 deepens typography. |
| Dense operational interface | Arc02; task completion, data hierarchy, state/recovery and keyboard use; Arc03 adds numerical/data craft. |
| Public-service form | Arc02; content clarity, errors, interrupted work, recovery and focus/reading order. |
| Image-led collection | Arc03; art direction, focal hierarchy, orientation, accessible exploration and reduced motion. |
| Multilingual experience | Arc02, extended in Arc03; explicitly named second script/direction, expansion/shaping and knowledgeable review. |
| Existing non-C&B identity | Arc01 onward; preserve supplied tokens/type/identity and justify local changes; Arc03 tests mode/semantic-change propagation. |

Choose exact locale/device/browser/tool coverage with the first dependent slice,
not in a separate project-wide research phase. A shared specimen may exercise
several families if its inputs and observations genuinely cover them. The project
still covers all six families and seven capabilities; it does not promise every
possible combination of family, locale, browser and mode.

Review factual/numerical correctness, functionality/accessibility, task fit,
visual craft, expression, evidence and maintainability separately. A screenshot
or parser pass is not behavioral proof; AI agreement is not user research or
expertise. Use actual browser/keyboard/assistive checks for claimed behavior,
and appropriate designer/script expertise for the promised expert judgments.
Record who reviewed what and unavailable participants/expertise honestly; those
gaps remain open where required. No fabricated personas, findings or conformance.

Use fresh-context package tasks to verify navigation and usefulness as routes
land, plus a connected final demonstration. Reuse independently accepted evidence
only after checking revision applicability; rerun changed/affected checks and
system composition. Final composition is not a second whole-project review.
Controlled comparisons, repeated/blinded runs or a baseline-versus-new study are
needed when making comparative effectiveness claims, not as a prerequisite to
every bounded guide edit. “Top-tier” remains an ambition, not a beauty score or
an unsupported claim of superiority.
