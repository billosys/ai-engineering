# Unified Design Practice — 2.0 Target

## Purpose and authority

Develop the design skill into a designer's working resource across intent,
content, perception, expression, interaction and implementation. The Operator
assigned the 2.0 effort to Project11 on 2026-10-08, separate from foundation
repair and sharing the Project09 audit. Planning lives on branch `planning`, in
`.worktrees/planning/project11-unified-design-practice/`; implementation belongs
in the source checkout. This is an initial roadmap, not an implementation handoff.

The shared architecture proposal is Project09's
[release synthesis](../project09-design-audit/artifacts/audit-modernization-synthesis.md),
with its seven capabilities and proposed evaluation. The
[audit findings](../project09-design-audit/artifacts/audit-results-design.md) remain
owned by Project09. [Project10](../project10-design-foundations/project-plan.md)
provides the accepted corrected package and its dispositions. Consume both
directly and pin the revisions at intake; do not copy or restate the original
audit as though it observed the future 1.5 package.

## Scope and definition of done

Provide connected, actionable routes for framing, exploring, composing,
specifying, building, critiquing, validating and maintaining designs. Preserve
the emphasis on intentional relationships, content, expressive coherence and
craft; demonstrate that these support different contexts and identities.

The proposed initial operational scope is web, product and editorial design.
The roadmap develops problem framing; IA/content/interaction; richer visual
craft; inclusive/global practice; system governance and handoff; evaluation;
and a usable knowledge instrument. The [ledger](ledger.md) measures capabilities
and their composition. A larger reading collection alone does not satisfy it.

Full native-platform specialization, print, spatial/industrial design and full
service-design practice remain proposed future extensions, subject to Operator
scope decisions. AI-assisted work does not make specialized AI-product patterns
mandatory for every task. Source acquisition must be question-led and preserve
rights/provenance. Borrow methods from external systems without importing their
visual identities or treating them as universal authority.

## Evaluation and open decisions

Use the synthesis's six task families as a starting proposal: editorial,
dense operational interface, public-service form, image-led collection,
multilingual experience and an existing non-C&B identity. Specify supported
environments, task inputs, package/model/tool conditions, repetition and review
criteria before trials. Judge factual correctness, accessibility/function,
task fit, craft, expression, evidence and maintainability separately.

Resolve concrete domain/locale coverage, source priorities, knowledge packaging
and the trial contract before their dependent assignments. Incorporate the
Project10 corpus and compatibility decisions only when actually recorded.
The ambition is top-tier usefulness; no universal design-superiority claim is
accepted without bounded demonstrations, expert judgment and use evidence.
