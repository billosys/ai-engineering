the isolated design package, and use the real LFE site to demonstrate contextual
application with supplied identity. Deliver a usable light/dark colour-role
foundation and editorial reading/navigation journey, preserving actual content,
code, routes and brand. These are early operational design improvements, not a
complete IA/product redesign or all of2.0. No source-fidelity recertification.

utilities/gold ramp/Pagefind roles. Inspect actual loaded styles, states and
consumers; do not equate renaming unused variables with fixing the site.
LFE's supplied gold wordmark/mascot and local Newsreader/system-font intent remain.
Tasks inferred from actual navigation/content, not user research: newcomers find
Learn/Use, practitioners distinguish download versions, readers follow an article
and return to context. Label those assumptions; no fabricated personas/analytics.
