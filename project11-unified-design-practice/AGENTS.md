# Project11 delivery instructions

Read [project-plan.md](project-plan.md) and [ledger.md](ledger.md) before planning
or implementation. Parent planning-worktree instructions apply. This project
remains planned in the Two-Contributor Workflow; neither CRC nor Expedited Mode
is enabled. Planning lives here; source work needs an issued exact-scope prompt.

- Plan the next useful source/package increment, not a separate discovery,
  evidence, correction and validation pipeline. Preserve all seven capabilities,
  six task families and ten final criteria while sizing slices for review headroom.
- Each normal delivery names its source paths, user task, protected useful
  behavior/examples, checks and integration destination. Research answers the
  questions needed to make that change; unresolved source claims remain explicit.
- Consume independently accepted Project10 inputs at their actual scope. Check
  drift and coordinate overlapping mutations. Final acceptance still needs the
  complete accepted foundation; no re-certification of its unchanged full corpus.
- Include relevant behavior, inclusion, expert review and isolated package use
  where the capability lands. Preserve good teaching during generalization;
  absence of forbidden presets alone does not establish usefulness.
- Reuse applicable accepted evidence; rerun changed/affected checks. Prefer
  canonical records and one compact batch note, not duplicated historical payloads
  or reporting-only correction loops. Keep material failures and unknowns visible.
- At close name the accepted source revision and usable improvement, or the
  precise blocker/owner. Follow up process observations in existing close records;
  do not extend Project13's completed trial or create another measurement phase.
- Preserve issued prompts and unrelated work. Recommendations are not decisions;
  independent acceptance and Operator release gates remain binding.
