## Connected corrections

1. **Rendering versus perception.** Local stacking contexts are atomic paint groups; z-index integers are not physical or perceived distances, semantic priority or compositor-layer identities. Overlap needs actual painted geometry/alpha and relevant comparable contexts. A shadow, transform or high integer does not guarantee a perceived plane, importance, clickability or a complete widget. Preserve the cards' own useful paint/perception distinctions consistently in all copies. No mandatory3–5 planes, token hierarchy or stronger shadow for every important element.

2. **Contour ownership and regions.** CSS authored borders and perceptual boundaries are different. CSS collapsed table borders lie on grid lines with conflict resolution; separate cells and gaps do not guarantee a perceptual cure. Keep either appropriate table model, no obligatory8/16px gaps or collapse ban. A selected binary image partition needs a domain and boundary convention; its complement or topology does not choose perceived ownership or prove every region one role. Figure-ground factors are reported contextual tendencies, not no-learning proof or universal brighter/red/convex winner. Retain goblet, honeycomb, Douris, Moore/Archipenko and related source examples without inventing repairs to quoted OCR.

3. **Flat, framed and spatial treatments.** Attribute Arnheim's no-flat-image account rather than establish every picture's unavoidable depth. Flat style can contain shallow planes, overlaps and3/4 icons, and can support maps/navigation. Do not equate it with all Material/Apple generations, minimum radius, maximum recognizability or viewer exclusion as a usability failure. Framing/window conventions can nest. overflow and crop are geometry, not exclusive perceptual ownership. Keep expressive ambiguity when intended; no universal UI anti-pattern or calibrated double-well switching mechanism follows.

4. **Compositing and transparency.** A visible-through reading differs from alpha, filtering and blend operations; none guarantees the percept. For a stated same-space scalar source-over example, a cf+(1-a)cb is a convex mixture for a in[0,1]. Multiply is a chosen blend function, not all pigment mixing, and light mixing is separate. Resolve real backdrop/pairs. Hard cast shadows can exist (e.g. a point-light model); the reported hard/soft Hering demonstration is not a universal shadow classifier. Separate flat object colours, painted overlays, actual physical transmission and cue interpretation.

5. **Projection and scale.** Use camera-axis depth Z in the stated pinhole model, not arbitrary Euclidean distance: projected linear extent fL/Z, area proportional1/Z² under defined plane/orientation. Direction families have their own vanishing points; only the appropriate horizontal families lie on a level horizon, and some directions have no finite point. Eye/apex is not the image vanishing point.3D→2D projection is not invertible; plane homographies/cross-ratios require nondegenerate stated domains. Multiple vanishing points can be geometrically consistent, not automatically ambiguity/relativism. No historical-once, all-camera, strongest-cue or cultural symbolism claim is newly verified.

6. **Parallel and exact isometric models.** Distinguish broader represented Arnheim usage from strict engineering equal-axis foreshortening. Parallelism alone does not establish exact isometry or true lengths; some directions collapse. Orthographic projection is not necessarily an invertible planar affine map. With stated Rx/Rz order and no perspective, Rx60/Rz45 projects unit axes to approximately.790569,.790569,.866025 (2:1 diamond); exact equal foreshortening uses Rx54.7356103/Rz45, all approximately.816497 (sqrt3 diamond). A single transformed face is not a constructed3D solid. Keep existing CSS examples explicitly illustrative; no universal viewpoint/navigation prohibition or mandatory mode purity.

7. **Stereo and geometric inference.** Distinct per-eye delivery, physical eye geometry and an actual comfortable stereo percept differ. Ordinary single-view presentation differs from stereo delivery on a flat substrate. In a stated symmetric midline model theta(Z)=2atan(b/(2Z)); define signed disparity relative to fixation Zf, delta=theta(Z)-theta(Zf). With b=.065,Zf1, Z.5 gives+.06484025rad and Z2 gives-.03247999; small-distance bd/D² is an approximation with conditions. No universal atan(d/D) formula or identity between eye and motion parallax. Preserve random-dot/eye-alternation examples as reports; do not invent device/comfort results.

8. **Gradients, texture and magnitude.** Monotone, regular and constant derivative differ: derivative of1/Z is−1/Z². Gradients need not produce depth or uniquely identify relief/concavity; the halo/tilted-cardboard counterexamples remain. No fitted area exponent transfers automatically to depth, typography, spacing or chart scales; chosen psi=kI^.7 gives2^.7≈1.6245 without fitting or mandatory compensation. Gaussian blur smooths, not resizes texture elements; fixed repeat sizes are not a spatial gradient. Defocus can occur in front or behind focus and differs from atmospheric scattering. Attenuation needs its medium/model; opacity need not lighten/desaturate. Seventeen wallpaper groups need doubly periodic planar symmetry assumptions, not all finite textures. Screen-down means increasing y in the stated convention; ground/horizon assumptions do not rank page content.

9. **Cue combination and UI use.** Bayesian combination needs likelihoods, prior and normalization; conditional independence is an assumption. A duplicated event E&E=E is not independent new evidence: prior.5/LR4 yields.8, not the erroneous squared-LR.94117647. Reliability is not simply the prior. Cues can conflict, reinforce or be deliberately stylized; no all-scene ranking, mandatory shadow/blur bundle or redundancy=accessibility guarantee. Retain supplied task/identity, actual states, semantics and relevant rest/fallback use. Inversion alone proves no contrast failure (black/white stays21:1).
