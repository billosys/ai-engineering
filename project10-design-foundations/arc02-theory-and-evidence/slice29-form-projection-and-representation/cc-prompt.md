## Root-resolved substantive corrections

1. **Projection and visibility.** Orthographic P(x,y,z)=(x,y) is linear. Perspective P=(x/z,y/z), z>0, is not: P(v)=P(2v), rather than 2P(v). A homogeneous matrix followed by division does not make the final Cartesian map linear. Orthographic rays are parallel and normal to the image plane; an object face can still be tilted and foreshortened. A finite candle is not an orthographic ray construction. Preserve the shadow example but separate front-facing aspect from parallel rays. Egyptian composite views need not implement one camera projection. Projection does not by itself decide visibility; opaque occlusion, wireframe and transparency are separate conditions. Retinal images and authored pictorial representations are different levels.

2. **Constancy and magnitude.** Shape constancy, size constancy and magnitude estimation are distinct. The reported Bower infant result does not independently prove innateness here. Motion parallax needs an appropriate viewpoint/object relation; not all movement qualifies, and the early-film fear account is not a parallax demonstration. A chosen model psi=k I^.7 gives 2^.7 approximately1.6245, but no fitted exponent or universal font/icon/spacing/chart compensation follows. Keep declared data encodings faithful to their quantities. Retain the current foundations qualifications on fitted Stevens/Weber models when those inputs remain unchanged.

3. **Size and roles.** Preserve the 13/15/17/22/31 example with actual adjacent ratios1.1538,1.1333,1.2941,1.4091. They are not all1.12–1.15, nor proof of a violated minimum ratio or a mandatory sqrt(2) system. Select role distinctions using content, script, actual face, optical fit and wrapping. Heading semantics are not defined by their visual sizes; icon sizing can answer optical context as well as priority. Preserve pharaoh, fox/crow, person/building and one-inch photograph examples without claiming measured dimensions never matter.

4. **Simplicity and formal analogies.** Praegnanz and simplicity are represented tendencies, not universal beauty or unique perceptual solutions. No prerequisites in a card does not prove innateness. MDL depends on model plus data and a code; Kolmogorov complexity is not a measured brain process. DRY concerns authoritative knowledge, not a ban on repeated UI. A Riemann sum approximates an integral value; it does not generically turn pieces into a smooth function. Interpolation is a different operation. Taylor approximations need conditions; differential calculus is not any staged refinement. A homeomorphism preserves topology, not smoothness, geometry or meaning. No specific gauge-invariance result about pictures was established.

5. **Development and construction.** Attribute differentiation, fusion and sculptural development to the historical account rather than prescribe a universal skill/cultural ladder or diagnose seams, facets, teachers or stakeholders. Continuous contours and assembled parts can both work. Preserve the fish counterexample, Picasso/Matisse lines, Horse, Assyrian five legs, ball speculation and Michelangelo/Bernini. Three axes do not become six spatial dimensions by counting categories. An ideal curve is one-dimensional even when embedded in 2D/3D; finite wire has material thickness. CSS radius rounds a 2D box; transformed faces are not automatically a volume or cognitive result. Keep additive and low-poly construction available.

6. **Frames and orientation.** Specify the reference frame. A square rotated90 degrees has identical occupied geometry; at45 degrees the same shape can support a contextual diamond reading. Other symmetries differ. Eye/image axes, environmental axes and body/gravity cues can conflict. Do not require all primary content orthogonal and secondary content oblique, or every reference frame explicitly drawn. Cat-cortex, Attneave, Witkin and developmental-test accounts remain reported and unassessed. Obliqueness is not a calibrated force, urgency or angle-response law; an italic design may change letter construction as well as slant.

7. **CSS and authored depth.** writing-mode changes flow and glyph orientation, not merely image rotation. Block/inline axes depend on writing mode; Flexbox main/cross axes also depend on flex-direction. Nested z-index contexts are atomic paint groups, not a semantic-priority or perceived-distance scale. Transparent overlap, alpha compositing and blend modes are distinct. Do not promise overlap always outweighs proximity or all depth must originate in a physical3D model. Preserve Sethos/Isis, Picasso and the music quotation as reported comparisons, without a universal chord-intensity law.

8. **Medium, representation and task.** Medium affordances do not mean every pencil makes outlines, brush makes disks or clay makes spheres; available motion does not require every film/stage work to move. Preserve the quoted Degas dancer case while qualifying the inference. Reusing a supplied identity across media can be appropriate. A photograph, screenshot, diagram or complete reference can each serve a real task. Preserve the medical quotation about exact proportions, angles and textures; do not claim optical accuracy is irrelevant. Familiarity may influence reading, but it does not guarantee eventual normalization or diagnose user fixation.

9. **Abstraction and depth frameworks.** Abstraction is not one universal axis of detail, maturity or skill. Attribute judgments about ornament and empty formalism; do not equate flat/minimal with quality. The0–100 plane/depth account is a schematic source framework, not a calibrated law of every picture. Flat treatments can contain shallow cues and remain usable. Monophony/polyphony/homophony is an analogy, not a superior developmental ladder or literal spatial coordinate.

10. **Structured equivalence.** A homomorphism requires specified structures/operations and can lose important distinctions: f(n)=0 preserves integer addition while collapsing all integers. Shared descriptive words alone do not define an algebra of visual meaning. Fixed pitch translation p_i→p_i+k preserves directed intervals under that chosen model, not every register, timbre, tuning or expressive meaning. Interface contracts may include appearance when the task requires it. Structural interpretation is one lens alongside recognition, exact text/state, metrics, audience and optical record, not an exclusive success criterion.
