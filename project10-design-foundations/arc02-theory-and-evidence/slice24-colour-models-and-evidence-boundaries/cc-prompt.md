   No fixed hue-family→OKLCH angle table or spectral-red-to-red physical theorem.

   schematic cylindrical coordinates from Euclidean perceptual metrics and
   independent realizability. Hue is undefined at zero chroma; admissible chroma
   depends on hue/value/reference/material/gamut. Do not imply unbounded realizable
   surface colour. Historical pigment maxima aren't all-time maxima. In a Euclidean
   illustration, constant r is cylindrical; sphere boundary instead satisfies
   r²+(z-z0)²=R², so radius depends on height. Neither diagram guarantees balance.
   Historical black/white rhetoric isn't a physical claim about every material/

3. Value/value-scale: photometry measures light, not exact perceptual equality.
   Distinguish approximate calibrated Munsell V, relative luminance, perceived
   brightness/lightness, CIELAB L* and OKLCH L. No fixed exponent of 0.33 or universal cube-root
   calibration. Even a stipulated power law doesn't make equal output increments
   geometric input increments. Preserve the historical 1–9 example, but not as the
   only allowed samples or a physical achievable-extreme law. Decimal values

   useful worked examples as historical/current-conditional arithmetic, not a

   Temporal Maxwell-disc averaging, material mixture, linear-light, CSS encoded/OKLCH
