## Connected corrections and teaching

1. **Attributes and models.** Distinguish luminance, perceived brightness/lightness,
source/pictorial value, chroma, saturation and purity with named reference/model.
HSL S/L, HSV S/V, OKLCH L/C, relative sRGB luminance and CIE appearance attributes
are not interchangeable scales. Saturation is relative to own brightness; chroma
uses a similarly illuminated reference white. Keep ordinary vividness/intensity
wording scoped. Do not call hue/chroma/lightness universal independent axes or
HSL L perceptually uniform. RGB/CMY/RYB primaries, mixtures and complements require
specified additive/subtractive/artist models, whitepoint and device/material;
three chosen primaries do not span every physically visible colour. A complement
across a selected wheel need not neutralize in every interpolation/mixing process.

2. **Defined witnesses.** In encoded sRGB, HSL red and lime both L=.5 but linear
relative luminances .2126/.7152 and white contrast approximately3.99847677/1.37219028.
Encoded .5 decodes to approximately .21404114, not linear .5. For OKLCH L=.6,C=.1,
h30/210 Cartesian a/b midpoint is near zero chroma; a chosen increasing polar
hue path midpoint h120 retains .1. Specify mixing/interpolation space and route.
These are analytic models, not observer measurements or all-display guarantees.

3. **Constancy and illumination.** Brightness/colour constancy are conditional
appearance phenomena, not perfect scene invariance, reliable reflectance recovery
or CSS side effects. A scalar I=.2 can be illuminant .2×reflectance1 or .4×.5;
observation alone does not choose a decomposition. Do not manufacture chronology
that Arnheim1954 predates every vonKries formulation or claim a named CAT occurred/
did not occur in an unread original. In a specified linear-response model,
y=M^-1 D Mx with D=diag(Mw_dest/Mw_source), nonzero source channel responses and
invertible M maps the chosen source white to target white. Fixed M/D is a linear
construction, not a complete biological adaptation model or nonlinear full-colour
pipeline. Engineered image filtering and contextual appearance are distinct.

4. **Tonal contrast and context.** Rendering/lighting/pigment/appearance/described
value differ. Darker/lighter surrounding comparisons and figure-ground tendencies
are contextual, not one universal law or a proof of all neural pathways. Preserve
the simultaneous-contrast demonstrations and their conditions; do not treat an
arbitrary CSS HSL change as a measured perceptual compensation. Keep hue-family/
lightness/chroma distinctions and actual font/size/state/background pairs for
contrast decisions. Inverting black/white preserves21:1; inversion can change
other pairings and semantic roles, but cannot universally cause failure. Give
actual conceptual readability/pair advice before separately flagging absent
rendered interface evidence. No CSS luminance/luma shorthand is a WCAG guarantee.

5. **Warmth, palettes and expression.** Artistic warm/cool character is relative
and contextual; CCT/blackbody/source spectrum and a hue-wheel metaphor differ.
CSS hue-rotate is a specified matrix, not a Kelvin transform or uniform hue shift:
a neutral .5,.5,.5 stays neutral in the stated matrix even at−20deg. No all-red/
all-blue depth/weight/temperature, universal emotion or mandatorywarmCTA follows.
Preserve supplied brand identity/task; analogous/complementary harmony is an option,
not proof that nonharmonic palettes are bad, that every neutral must be tinted or
that six semantic roles violate one colour quota. Visible distinctions should
support actual meaning and adequate pairs; avoid decorative diversity for its own
sake without banning purposeful categorical colour. OKLCH palette construction
still needs output gamut/contrast/state inspection, not automatic equal-L safety.

6. **Glows, shadows and spatial readings.** Pictorial self-luminosity is not actual
emitted radiance or high luminance alone. Compositing luminosity blends transfer
specified luminance-like components; they do not automatically produce a glowing
percept. Alpha, multiply/screen/filter and pigment/light mixing remain separate.
Shadow shape, softness and light direction depend on model/material/geometry;
point lights can produce hard shadows. A shadow can be expressive, painted or
misleading, not a calibrated distance or semantic-priority measure. Flat/calm
methods and useful shadows both remain available. Coordinate accepted tonal-weight/
colour-weight/tonal-contrast and Slice30 transparency/depth accounts without
merging their source-specific identities.

7. **UI application and meaning.** Colour-only disabled/error states are insufficient
when colour is the sole meaning cue. Retain the real badge question contributors:
colour-constancy already discusses redundant encoding; saturation's own disabled
prescription needs repair. A small actual text/state/icon/shape distinction within
that application is a bounded correction, not a whole widget curriculum. A visible
“Error” label or equivalent meaningful cue is different from motion/colour alone;
actual keyboard/behavior/contrast/rendering outcomes are separate checks. No
lowering essential contrast to convey low risk, mandatory pale-disabled text or
universal agency/psychology diagnosis from appearance. CSS snippets may illustrate
choices only with actual effects/fallback limitations, no assumed screenshot proof.

8. **Attribution and concrete examples.** Preserve source quotations, paintings,
artist/experiment examples, historical Katz/Arnheim terminology and chapter context
as attributed reports. Removing inaccurate strict alias labels does not erase
historical discussion or assert the original book was wrong. Scope all repeated
Quick/Core/Rosetta/CommonConfusions/Relationships/VerificationNotes copies. Keep
frozen extraction confidence separate from current bounded review. No original
passage, historical date, physiological mechanism or empirical fit is invented.
