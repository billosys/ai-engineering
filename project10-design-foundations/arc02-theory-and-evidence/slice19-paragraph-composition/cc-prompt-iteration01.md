they are specified, then inherit as a computed length. Recommended replacement
shape (wording may vary while preserving the meaning):

> A percentage line-height computes from the font size of the element where
> it is specified, and the resulting length is inherited. A20px parent declaring
> 150% computes30px; a12px child inherits30px unless it overrides that value.
> If that child explicitly declares150%, it computes18px instead. A unitless1.5
> is inherited as a number, giving30px/18px on those parent/child font sizes.
