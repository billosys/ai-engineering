## Root-resolved connected correction recipes

1. **Forces, weight and balance.** Magnitude and direction permit a proposed vector representation; they do not establish physical units, calibrated perceptual weights, additive superposition or a universal observer response. Attribute the neural-field account to Arnheim. Keep the roving disk, moving frame, opposing rope pulls and magnetic-filings comparisons without claiming they prove perceptual energy. Balance can be a compositional goal; tension or disequilibrium can be expressive. Do not require every finished interface to minimize energy, privilege the centre or subordinate the periphery. Dominant/tonic language is a comparison within a tonal idiom, without a shared energy metric or an all-music law.

2. **Levers and location.** Physical torque is r×F, with magnitude rF sin(theta), or force times perpendicular lever distance. Qualitative visual counterbalancing need not be proportional to arbitrary pixel distance. Separate support/stability, prominence/interest and counterbalancing influence. A CSS document origin is not a compositional fulcrum. Preserve Manet's distant girl as the reported example: importance does not mean she appears physically larger. Remove mandatory bottom-right CTA and peripheral-elements-must-be-lighter rules.

3. **Tonal reversal.** The retained quotation says the black area must be LARGER than the white area to counterbalance it in the reported example. Correct the opposite account in current Core/metadata/errors. Distinguish physical area, apparent area and compositional weight. Remove the unsupported one-third-radius optics and universal equal-apparent-area reversal rule. Preserve the irradiation explanation as a reported account, without claiming a verified mechanism for every background/viewing condition.

4. **Vertical placement and padding.** For content height h in a borderless box with top padding p and bottom padding q, content-centre minus outer-centre is (p−q)/2. Thus24/32 gives−4px (up), while26/24 gives+1px (down). This is geometry, not an optical prescription. More bottom padding moves the content up; equal padding is not universally wrong. Separate the quoted bisection adjustment from which equal half appears longer, optical centring, actual glyph/line-box/frame geometry and reported larger bottom margins. The rose-window quotation says above centre; do not conclude downward displacement. Keep the3/S/B inversion, Greenough and World's Fair examples as contextual reports, not gravity proving all perception.

5. **Symmetry.** Define reflection using a specified set of points/features, transformation and invariance. Equal undefined aggregate weights of different forms do not establish reflection; a balanced composition can be asymmetric. Retain exact geometric rigour within that model, without proving perceived balance. A centred box or equal Grid tracks do not mirror arbitrary contents. Musical AA/AB repetition or sequence is not necessarily geometric reflection, universal stability or conserved weight. Do not introduce a false disk/rose-window inference.

6. **Compositional skeleton.** A perceived skeleton is not an authored12-column grid. A square's centre, axes and diagonals offer a useful comparison, but their common intersection does not produce multiple additional points. Remove generic browser-fold/top-third anchors and measured-force/stability guarantees for every format. Grid defines tracks; custom properties store values until used. Compare actual placed content rather than require all primary information at geometric anchors. Keep the reported experiment attributed and unassessed.

7. **Colour attributes and weight.** A red/blue example does not establish an entire rainbow ranking, independent additive weight dimensions, longer-wavelength cone causation, or warm-primary/cool-secondary semantic roles. Display colours need not have one wavelength. Brightness, lightness, chroma and contrast interact and are distinct quantities. CIE lightness is brightness relative to a similarly illuminated white; neither brightness nor WCAG relative luminance is physical mass. WCAG Y is not Munsell value or Oklab L, and Munsell value should not be called context-free absolute appearance. Preserve the bedcover, clown, Féré, Goldstein, Kandinsky and Albers accounts as reported. Claims of replication, failed replication or comparative reliability are not verified here.

8. **Conflicting weight accounts and dark themes.** ChapterVII's colour-weight card says darker is heavier; ChapterI's tonal/weight cards say brighter is heavier. Explicitly distinguish the represented accounts and contexts rather than choose a universal winner. Weight is compositional vocabulary, not a calibrated mass sum. Inversion or a black background alone does not diagnose fatigue or heaviness. Remove the required OKLCH L0.12–0.15 and blanket pure-black ban. Preserve supplied identity, theme roles, state pairs and actual text/geometry, with viewing conditions/preferences considered. Candidate values remain examples; no dark-theme usability/fatigue result was measured.

9. **Contrast.** Value difference is one visibility cue; chromatic difference can also distinguish boundaries. Squinting/greyscale are bounded diagnostics, not complete colour/accessibility proof. Weber describes fitted discrimination for a stimulus/task/range, not a derivation of WCAG or proof that equal ratios look equally contrasted. Separate brightness/lightness from WCAG relative luminance. WCAG2.2 normal/large AA thresholds are4.5/3 and AAA thresholds7/4.5, with the actual criterion exceptions. For opaque rendered sRGB, use (Llighter+.05)/(Ldarker+.05) after linearization; requirements alone do not ensure readability or hierarchy. Include relevant state/font/non-colour conditions. The inspected WCAG3 publication is a10September2026 WorkingDraft, not a normative APCA replacement. Retain APCA only as a scoped proposal if useful. Supporting text is not automatically legible at low contrast; inactive-control exceptions do not excuse ordinary labels/placeholders.

10. **CSS mechanics.** Opacity composites the element and descendants and does not always darken it: under a declared flat encoded-sRGB model, black at.4 over white gives grey.6; white at.4 over black gives.4. Opposite directions do not prove salience. Resolve actual rendered pairs after alpha/filtering. Requested font weight needs a supporting face;2rem or saturate(.3) alone proves no perceptual outcome. z-index is scoped paint order, not depth/priority. direction:ltr controls direction, not lateral neural bias or automatic RTL perceptual reversal. With zero margins, a100px positioned box in a400px containing block at left:50% has left edge200px and centre250px, not200px; supply actual alignment/context if centring is intended.

11. **Left and right.** Preserve Raphael mirroring and the rider/stage examples as reported contextual comparisons. Identification, importance, conspicuity, apparent size, reading direction, depicted movement and counterbalancing around an attention centre differ. Neural hypotheses and RTL reversal are not currently proven universal laws. Remove immutable left anchors and automatic full-mirroring recipes; preserve meaningful script/content/source/navigation order and relevant actual-use checks. Separate geometric symmetry from a possible contextual perceptual asymmetry instead of claiming a contradiction in the geometry itself.

12. **Tension and springs.** In an ideal Hookean model F=−kx and U=.5kx² with U(0)=0. At k=1, x1→2 doubles force magnitude1→2 but quadruples energy.5→2. Energy is not proportional to displacement, and visual tension has no established proportionality to angle or deformation here. Keep oblique/orthogonal, regular/pure-colour and maximum/minimum claims contextual to the source account. Preserve Kandinsky, VanDoesburg's windmills, Rausch, Müller-Lyer and Rembrandt as unverified reported examples. Not all visual dynamism has one cause, and no font-scale rule follows from tension.
