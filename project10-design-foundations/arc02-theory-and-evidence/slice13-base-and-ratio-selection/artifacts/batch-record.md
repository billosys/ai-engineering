## Independent arithmetic controls

Reproduced with Python `Fraction`, `Decimal` precision 50 and `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`:

- `r=Fraction(809,500)`; `r*r-r-1 = Fraction(-19,250000)`.
- `1/r = Fraction(500,809)`, which is unequal to `Fraction(309,500)` (the exact rational 0.618).
- For positive lengths `L>S`, let `x=L/S`. Then `(L+S)/L = 1+S/L = 1+1/x`; equating to `x` gives `x²=x+1`. The positive root is `φ=(1+√5)/2`; only this exact model has reciprocal `φ−1`.
- Base 18 with decimal `r=809/500`: step 1 is exactly 29.124; step 2 from base is 47.122632; half-even 3-place displays are 29.124 and 47.123. Reusing rounded step 1 is prohibited.
- Base 18 with exact φ at Decimal precision 50: step values are approximately 29.1246118 and 47.1246118; half-even displays are 29.125 and 47.125.
- Independent base-10 descending exact-decimal values for n=1,2,3 are approximately 6.18047, 3.81982, 2.36083. Brown's printed 6.180/3.819/2.360 remain attributed and unmodified.
- One valid non-lineage illustration is the selection card's explicitly hypothetical 16px, `r=1.25` candidate for inspection against dense-interface constraints. It is neither a default nor an observed rendering result. Brown's 18px Minion/1:1.618/190px contextual example and his later improvisation are preserved.
