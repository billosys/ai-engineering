card-to-source index and source-gap report, plus a portable design contract and

## Design boundaries

Binding decisions: all 233 cards receive full semantic review in the project;
remove C&B from the live skill with Git history only; its removal corrects a bug
and needs no compatibility bridge; Markdown sources use `sources/md/`; preserve
the skill's useful compositional ethos. The contract must distinguish these from
new proposed teaching wording and configurations, which CDC/Operator adopt later.

Slice01 is complete when its bounded evidence and proposed contract are accepted,
even if it discovers source gaps. That does not complete the project or make
dependent semantic checks ready. Record precise affected populations and next
actions; later source/readiness work must resolve required gaps. This separation
does not authorize reducing the approved review scope.
